THE IDEA BEHIND THE INTERACTION

How it works.

  1. 01

    Choose a size

    Enter megabytes, not megabits.

  2. 02

    Choose a speed

    Use megabytes per second to keep units consistent.

  3. 03

    Divide

    Size divided by speed gives seconds.
