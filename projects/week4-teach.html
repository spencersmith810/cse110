How it works.

    Find the drag factor

    Use the formula

LOOK CLOSER / LINE 16v=math.sqrt((m * g) / c) * (1-math.exp((-math.sqrt(m * g * c)/m)*t))

The exponential term changes with time; the square-root factor sets the limiting velocity.
