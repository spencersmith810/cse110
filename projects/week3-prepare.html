THE IDEA BEHIND THE INTERACTION

How it works.

  1. 01

    Add one year

    The entered age becomes the age at the next birthday.

  2. 02

    Convert dozens

    Multiply the number of dozens by twelve.

  3. 03

    Share cookies

    Divide the cookies by the entered number of people.

LOOK CLOSER / LINE 21div = (cookie/people)

The / operator returns the share per person. A zero divisor is not allowed.
