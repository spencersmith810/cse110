THE IDEA BEHIND THE INTERACTION

How it works.

  1. 01

    Read rider details

    Some ages trigger an extra passport question.

  2. 02

    Apply the passport

    A yes answer treats a 12–17-year-old as age 18 for these rules.

  3. 03

    Check shared rules

    Pair and solo requirements use different combinations of age and height.

LOOK CLOSER / LINE 24if height_one >= 36 and height_two >= 36:

Every pair must pass the minimum-height test before any of the age combinations are considered.
