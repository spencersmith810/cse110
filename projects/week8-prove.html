THE IDEA BEHIND THE INTERACTION

How it works.

  1. 01

    Choose a category

    Select Book of Mormon, Bible, or general vocabulary words.

  2. 02

    Read position clues

    A for loop compares each letter and reports exact or misplaced matches.

  3. 03

    Finish the quest

    The while loop limits attempts and a fast win can unlock a bonus guess.

LOOK CLOSER / LINE 230for index in range(len(secret_word)):

The loop visits each position in the secret word so the game can compare the guess letter at the same index.
