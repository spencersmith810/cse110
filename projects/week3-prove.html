THE IDEA BEHIND THE INTERACTION

How it works.

  1. 01

    Build the subtotal

    Multiply each price by its quantity, then add the results.

  2. 02

    Calculate tax

    Convert a percentage into a fraction and round the tax to two decimals.

  3. 03

    Make change

    Subtract the bill from the cash payment.

LOOK CLOSER / LINE 25taxamount = round(total * (tax / 100),2)
