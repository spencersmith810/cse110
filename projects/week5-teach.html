THE IDEA BEHIND THE INTERACTION

How it works.

  1. 01

    Read two percentages

    The grade and passing threshold are separate inputs.

  2. 02

    Check thresholds

    An if/elif ladder selects the first matching grade band.

  3. 03

    Add a sign

    A second ladder applies the exact plus/minus bands in the file.

LOOK CLOSER / LINE 54elif grade >= 87:

The conditions are ordered from highest to lowest so a grade lands in one band.
