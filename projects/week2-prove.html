THE IDEA BEHIND THE INTERACTION

How it works.

  1. 01

    Gather nine words

    Ask for an adjective, an animal, three verbs, and a few story details.

  2. 02

    Build the story

    Each f-string inserts an answer into a fixed story template.

  3. 03

    Add presentation

    Separator lines and blank lines make the output easier to read.
